Statistical Computing Software and Resources


R

R is free software available for download from the R homepage (click on "CRAN" on the left margin of the page). Download the Precompiled Binary Distribution for your operating system from a mirror near you. The online R documentation, via your default web browser, is obtained by typing "help.start()" on the window running R.

  • Time Series Analysis:
    • A detailed R overview of all of time series.
    • R News article that describes the basic details of the main time series packages (2002).
    • Package itsmr provides a subset of the functionality found in the Windows-based program ITSM from the textbook "Introduction to Time Series and Forecasting", 2002, by Peter J. Brockwell and Richard A. Davis.
    • Diethelm Wurtz's slides describing the Rmetrics packages for finance and econometrics (2008).

  • Spatial Statistics:
    • A detailed R overview of all of spatial statistics.
    • A list of major packages for spatial stats (2010).

    • Website of geoR, a package for geostatistical data analysis.
  • Code Snipets for Applied Statistics (mostly STAT 5302-5303):
    • Fancy pairs plot: scatterplots on lower diagonal, correlations on upper (with font size proportional to magnitude).
    • Sampling distributions and Central Limit Theorem.
    • Comparing two populations via two sample t-tests.
    • One way and two way ANOVA.
    • ANCOVA with the Glue data and the Golf data.
    • Linear Regression: Simple (Computer data) and Mutliple (Peruvian Indian data).
    • Poisson regression with the Horseshoe Crab data.
